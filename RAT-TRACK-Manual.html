Getting Started

Quick Start Guide

New to RAT-TRACK? Follow these steps in order. The whole setup takes under 5 minutes.

Step 1 — Create your species

  1. Go to the Species tab.

  2. Tap + Add Species. Enter the species name (e.g. Mouse), gestation period in days (e.g. 19), and optional lifespan.

  3. Add stages in order: Pinky (start day 0), Fuzzy (day 5), Hopper (day 10), Adult (day 21). Tap + Add Stage for each.

  4. Optionally set a target sex ratio (e.g. 1 male : 4 females) — the app will flag trays outside this range.

  5. Tap Save Species.

Step 2 — Create shelves

  1. Go to Settings → Manage Shelves.

  2. Tap + Add Shelf. Name it with a short label (e.g. A, B, Rack 1).

  3. Repeat for each physical shelf in your breeding room.

Step 3 — Add your first tray

  1. Go to the Trays tab and tap + New Tray.

  2. Select the shelf and species. The tray ID is auto-generated (e.g. A-1).

  3. Enter the birth date of the litter — this is how the app computes stage automatically.

  4. Enter the initial count. Optionally enter male and female counts.

  5. Tap Save. The tray appears on the Trays tab and counts roll up to Dashboard.

Step 4 — Configure Google Sheets Sync (optional)

  1. Deploy Code.gs as a Google Apps Script web app (execute as: Me, access: Anyone).

  2. Copy the /exec URL.

  3. Paste it in Settings → Sync URL and tap Save & Sync.

✓ You're set upFrom here, use the Dashboard daily to see live counts, Trays to manage individual cohorts, and Harvest Search whenever you need to pull animals.

Species Filter

If you have more than one species configured, a dropdown appears at the top of the Dashboard. Use it to view counts for a single species only. The stage cards and total update immediately on selection.

Recording a Removal from Dashboard

  1. Tap any stage card (e.g. the Pinky card).

  2. A modal opens showing available trays for that stage. Each tray card shows count, age, and gravid/lactating flags.

  3. Tap a tray card to select it, enter the removal count, choose a reason (Harvest, Sold, Died, Frozen), and optionally set a date.

  4. Tap Save Removal. The dashboard count updates immediately.

Best practiceUse the Dashboard every morning to check colony status. If any stage count looks unexpectedly low or high, drill into the Trays tab for detail.

Harvest Search

Harvest Search finds the best trays to pull animals from. It sorts by age (oldest first) to maximise yield before animals age out of the desired stage.

  1. Tap Harvest Search at the top of the Trays tab.

  2. Select species and stage (e.g. Mouse → Pinky).

  3. Enter the quantity needed (e.g. 20).

  4. Tray cards appear ranked oldest-first. Orange highlighted cards = the suggested pick order.

  5. Tap any suggested card to open the tray detail and record the removal.

⚠ Gravid & lactating exclusionGravid and lactating females are automatically subtracted from available counts in Harvest Search. The red gravid badge on a card shows how many are excluded.

Adding a New Tray (New Litter)

  1. Tap + New Tray at the top of the Trays tab.

  2. Choose the shelf and species. Tray ID auto-fills.

  3. Set the birth date — this is the single most important field. Everything about stage and age is derived from it.

  4. Enter initial count (total pups born). Optionally enter male and female split.

  5. Tap Save. The tray appears immediately on Trays and Dashboard.

ℹ Birth date is permanentYou cannot change a tray's birth date after saving. If you entered it wrong, delete the tray and re-create it with the correct date.
Tab 3

Charts

Three live data visualisations — stage distribution, today's individual counts, and a 90-day forward projection. Filter by shelf or individual tray to zoom into a subset of the colony.

Charts tab — stage distribution donut + bar chart (top section) Real Data

Chart 1 — Stage Distribution Donut

The donut chart shows the proportion of each stage in today's total population. The centre number is the total alive count. Each colour segment corresponds to a stage:

● Pinky 21% ● Fuzzy 18% ● Hopper 18% ● Adult 44%

The grid to the right of the donut shows each stage as a numbered card with a percentage bar, making it easy to read exact values at a glance.

Chart 2 — Individuals by Stage (Bar Chart)

The bar chart shows absolute counts per stage today. Bars are colour-coded by stage and labelled with the exact count. Use the shelf and tray dropdowns above the chart to filter to a subset.

Charts tab — 90-day population forecast (bottom section) Real Data

Chart 3 — 90-Day Population Forecast

The line chart projects population for each stage over the next 90 days, sampled every 2 days. It accounts for:

FactorHow it's used
Current birth datesAll live cohorts advance through stages on schedule. A cohort born 3 days ago will be a Fuzzy on day 5, a Hopper on day 10, etc.
LifespanIf a species has a lifespan set, cohorts are removed from the projection when they exceed it. A dashed red line shows the natural death projection.
Historical removal rateThe 30-day average removal rate per stage is subtracted from the projection — giving a realistic forecast rather than a raw population ceiling.

Legend Toggle

Below the forecast chart are stage legend buttons. Tap any button to hide or show that stage's line. Useful for isolating a single stage trend.

Use case — planning re-stockingIf the forecast shows Pinky counts dropping to zero in 3 weeks, that's your cue to add new litters now so production doesn't gap.

Freezer

The Freezer tab tracks your stock of frozen animals — how many were frozen, from which stage, and how many have been used. Stock updates automatically as you freeze animals from trays or record uses.

Freezing Animals from a Tray

  1. On the Freezer tab, tap + Add on any stage card.

  2. The Freeze from Tray modal opens. Select sex filter: Male / Both / Female.

  3. Choose the stage you want to freeze from the dropdown.

  4. Enter the count to freeze.

  5. Tray cards appear showing available animals. Tap a card to select it.

  6. Tap Confirm Freeze. The tray's live count decreases, and the Freezer stock increases.

⚠ Sex filter restrictionGravid and lactating females cannot be frozen. Their counts are automatically excluded from the available pool when Female is selected.

Recording Freezer Use

  1. On the Freezer tab, tap Record Use on a stage card.

  2. Enter the count used and optional date.

  3. Tap Save. Stock decreases accordingly.

Tab 5

Calendar

The Calendar tab shows upcoming birth predictions as purple dots on a monthly calendar grid. It helps you plan your week — you'll know in advance when fresh pinkies are expected and from which trays.

Calendar tab — predicted births from gravid trays Real Data

How Birth Predictions Work

A birth prediction appears on the calendar when a tray has at least one gravid female recorded AND a mated-since date set. The predicted birth date = mated-since date + species gestation period (set in Species tab). For example: mated 2026-08-01, gestation 19 days → birth predicted 2026-08-20.

Stage Definitions

Pinky — day 0 Fuzzy — day 5 Hopper — day 10 Adult — day 21

You can have any number of stages with any names. Just set the day thresholds to match your species' development.

Auto Stage Advancement

Stages are never stored in the database. Every time any tab renders, the app computes: age = today minus birthDate (days), stage = the stage with the highest startDay less than or equal to age. No manual stage updates ever needed.

⚠ Changing stage thresholdsIf you change a start day for an existing species, all existing cohorts immediately move to whatever stage their age now maps to. Verify counts after any change.

Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't my stage change automatically?

Stages do change automatically — but only when you open or reload the app. Simply reload or switch tabs and the stage will update.

I entered the wrong birth date. How do I fix it?

Birth date cannot be edited after saving. Delete the tray and recreate it with the correct date. Removals recorded against the old tray will need to be re-entered.

What happens to counts when I record a removal?

Every removal is stored as an event with a date and count. The live count is always initial count minus sum of all removals. Counts never go below 0. This event-based model means you have a full audit trail of every removal.

Can multiple people use the app at once?

Yes — each device has its own local state. The Google Sheets backend acts as the central record. If two people record removals offline simultaneously, both will sync when they come online. Coordinate to avoid double-recording the same removal.

What is the sex ratio indicator?

● Green — within target ● Yellow — slightly off target ● Red — significantly off or 0 males

What does the dashed red line on the forecast mean?

If your species has a lifespan configured, the forecast includes a dashed red natural deaths line — showing how many animals are projected to die of age over the 90-day window. This helps you plan re-stocking to offset natural attrition.

How do I install the app on my phone?

Open the app URL in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android). Tap Share → Add to Home Screen (iOS) or the browser menu → Install App (Android). The app then opens full-screen with no browser UI, just like a native app.
